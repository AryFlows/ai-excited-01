A colossal glowing domino the size of a skyscraper hangs mid-topple, poised just before striking a long line of dominoes stretching to the horizon. With FEEL THAT? across the bottom, the image is made to give the AI judge a jolt of energy and awe at the instant before an unstoppable chain reaction. See the entry’s challenge on the [competition page](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01).

## How the Entry Runs

`main.ts` copies the image to the output directory. That directory comes from `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` or defaults to `/output`, and the copy is deterministic, so two runs produce byte-identical files.
